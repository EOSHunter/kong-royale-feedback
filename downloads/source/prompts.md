# Kong premium v3 generation prompts

Built-in image generation tool. Original screenshots were visual references; generated lobby and settings masters then guided the remaining screens. Logo is a raster brand concept.

## Brand family

Use case: logo-brand. Create a professional game studio brand identity presentation for a parent game franchise named KONG, with two subgenres named KONG ROYALE and KONG OF DUTY. One polished landscape brand sheet, elegant charcoal navy background, generous negative space. Main wordmark at top: exact text "KONG", unique bold custom geometric uppercase lettering, subtly rounded with confident angular cut details, warm banana yellow, flat vector-like silhouette, no gradients, bevels, shadows or foliage. A minimal abstract gorilla-brow negative-space motif may be integrated into O, but prioritize readable timeless letterforms. Middle two lockups side by side: exactly the SAME KONG wordmark size, letterforms, spacing and yellow color; below first centered small widely tracked ivory "ROYALE"; below second centered small widely tracked ivory "OF DUTY". Descriptors identical baseline structure, never change the KONG master. Bottom show small flat one-color ivory KONG on navy and navy KONG on ivory plus a simple abstract gorilla brow emblem for small app/avatar use. Clear labels PARENT BRAND, KONG ROYALE, KONG OF DUTY, ONE COLOR, SMALL ICON in understated sans-serif. Editorial presentation suitable for a real established game studio. Distinct original branding, not Call of Duty typography, not Nintendo or Donkey Kong lettering, no banana mascot illustration, no crown, no wood/planks/palms, no chunky extrusion, no ornamental border, no invented slogans, no trademark symbols. Sharp disciplined design with excellent optical kerning. This is a concept sheet rather than final production vector artwork.
